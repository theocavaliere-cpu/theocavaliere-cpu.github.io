FunFacts Uploader

A personal tool that publishes short science videos to the FunFactsToKnow YouTube channel.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

  • FunFacts Uploader is used only by the owner of the FunFactsToKnow channel, to upload that channel's own videos.
  • It requests a single Google permission: uploading videos to YouTube (youtube.upload).
  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and does not read your other YouTube or Google data.
  • The access token Google issues is stored only on the owner's computer and is never sent anywhere except Google.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Videos are uploaded under the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
